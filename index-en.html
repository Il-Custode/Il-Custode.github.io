Author & DM

About Me

I have been a Dungeon Master and tabletop roleplayer since 1998, and for more than twenty-five years I have kept a constant passion for Dungeons & Dragons and for creating original content designed for real use at the table. Over time, I have developed many kinds of adventures, from short one-shots to side quests, from narrative modules to broader campaign ideas, always with special attention to the overall quality of play.

As an author, I like building material that feels evocative but also practical: content that is ready to use, easy to read, and genuinely helpful for the DM. Roleplay has always been one of the aspects I enjoy most, but over the years I have also worked a lot on homebrew systems, narrative structure, encounters, settings, and tools able to support very different tones, from serious to lighter, from mysterious to more adventurous.

THE ARCANE SHOP was born from that very experience: turning years of ideas, sessions, table testing, and design work into polished and versatile products for people looking for D&D material that combines atmosphere, playability, and identity.

Live and Online Sessions

Alongside content creation, I am also available as a Dungeon Master for Hire for live sessions in Milan and online. For interested groups, I offer a free introductory mini one-shot lasting 2–3 sessions, ideal for creating the right chemistry at the table before moving into longer adventures.

Editorial Lines

THE ARCANE SHOP will grow around three main editorial lines: quick adventures, high-atmosphere modules, and practical tools for DMs and groups who want to expand their game experience.

One-shots and Short Quests

Self-contained adventures and side quests that are easy to add to existing campaigns or play across just a few sessions.

Narrative Modules

Content with strong identity, curated atmosphere, and a clear structure, designed to support the DM without slowing down the table.

Homebrew and Tools

Original ideas, support systems, practical prompts, and useful material to expand your game with more depth and flexibility.
