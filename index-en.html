About Me

I have been a Dungeon Master and tabletop roleplayer since 1998, and for more than twenty-five years I have kept the same strong passion for Dungeons & Dragons and for creating original material designed to be used in real play.

Over time, I have developed all kinds of content: short one-shots, side quests, narrative modules, and broader campaign ideas, always with a strong focus on the quality of the experience at the table.

As an author, I like creating material that feels evocative but also practical: content that is easy to read, ready to use, and genuinely helpful for Dungeon Masters. Roleplay has always been one of the parts of the game I value most, but over the years I have also worked extensively on homebrew systems, encounter structure, storytelling, settings, and tools that can support very different tones, from serious to lighter, from mysterious to adventurous.

THE ARCANE SHOP was born from that experience: turning years of ideas, sessions, table testing, and design work into polished, versatile products for people looking for D&D material that combines atmosphere, playability, and identity.

Alongside content creation, I am also available as a Dungeon Master for hire for live sessions in Milan and online. For interested groups, I offer a free introductory mini one-shot lasting 2–3 sessions, ideal for building the right table chemistry before moving into longer adventures.

Editorial Direction

Editorial Lines

THE ARCANE SHOP is growing around three main directions: quick adventures, atmosphere-driven modules, and practical tools for Dungeon Masters and groups who want to deepen and improve their game experience.

One-shots & Short Quests

Self-contained adventures and side quests that are easy to drop into existing campaigns or play across a small number of sessions, without losing atmosphere or identity.

Narrative Modules

Content with strong personality, curated atmosphere, and a clear structure, designed to support the DM without slowing down the flow of the table.

Homebrew & Tools

Original ideas, support systems, practical prompts, and useful resources built to expand the game with deeper and more versatile material.
