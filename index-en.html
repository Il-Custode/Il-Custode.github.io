About Me

A Dungeon Master and roleplayer since 1998, I have spent over twenty-five years cultivating a constant passion for Dungeons & Dragons and for creating original content designed for real play at the table.

As an author, I love building material that is both evocative and practical: ready-to-use content, easy to read, and genuinely useful for Dungeon Masters.

THE ARCANE SHOP was born from this very experience: transforming years of ideas, sessions, playtesting, and design work into polished, versatile products for those seeking D&D material that combines atmosphere, playability, and identity.

In addition to content creation, I am also available as a Dungeon Master for hire for live sessions in Milan and online. For interested groups, I offer a free introductory 2–3 session mini one-shot, ideal for building the right table chemistry before starting longer campaigns.
