Dungeon Master for Hire

Curated, immersive, and personalized sessions for groups who want to experience Dungeons & Dragons with an expert Dungeon Master, both live in Milan and online.

A premium service for groups who want to play well

Alongside the creation of adventures and editorial content, I offer a Dungeon Master for hire service designed for groups looking for curated, immersive sessions built with real attention to pacing, atmosphere, and table engagement.

This service is suitable both for new groups who want to start with confident guidance and for experienced players looking for a more refined, coherent, and memorable experience, either online or live in Milan.

For interested groups, I offer a free 2–3 session introductory mini one-shot, useful for getting to know each other, finding the right campaign tone, and understanding together whether my DMing style is the right fit for your table.

Group rates:
💻 Online: €50 per session (3–4 hours)
🏰 Live in Milan: €80 per session (3–4 hours)
📖 5-session mini campaign: €220 online / €350 live
