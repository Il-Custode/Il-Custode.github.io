Dungeon Master for Hire

Curated, immersive, and personalized sessions for groups who want to experience Dungeons & Dragons with an expert Dungeon Master, both live in Milan and online.

Premium Service

A guided game experience built with real care

Alongside the creation of adventures and editorial content, I offer a Dungeon Master for hire service designed for groups who want to sit at the table and enjoy a session built with care, pacing, and real attention to engagement.

This is not just about “running a game,” but about guiding the group through a more structured, immersive, and enjoyable experience, with careful attention to atmosphere, timing, narrative moments, and the overall fun of the table.

The service is suitable both for new groups looking to start with confident guidance and for experienced players who want a more refined, coherent, and memorable experience, either online or live in Milan.

For interested groups, I offer a free 2–3 session introductory mini one-shot, useful for getting to know each other, finding the right tone, and understanding together whether my way of running the game is truly the right fit for your table.

Group rates:
💻 Online: €50 per session (3–4 hours)
🏰 Live in Milan: €80 per session (3–4 hours)
📖 5-session mini campaign: €220 online / €350 live

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to be experienced players?

No. The service is also designed for new groups or players who want to approach Dungeons & Dragons with clear guidance and a more structured introduction.

Can we play online?

Yes. The service is available both online and live in Milan, depending on what works best for the group.

Do you also work with already formed groups?

Yes. In fact, that is one of the easiest situations to organize, because it allows me to shape a proposal right away around the number of players and the tone you want.

How does the free introductory mini one-shot work?

It is a short 2–3 session introductory experience designed to let us get to know each other, test the chemistry at the table, and decide whether to continue together with a longer format.

How long does a session last?

A session usually lasts between 3 and 4 hours, but the duration can be discussed together depending on the group, the type of adventure, and the players’ availability.

Can we contact you just to ask for information?

Absolutely. Even if you have not decided yet, you can simply write to understand whether the service fits your group and which format might work best for you.
