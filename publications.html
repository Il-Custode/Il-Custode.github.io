Editorial direction

How the catalog is growing

THE ARCANE SHOP is not meant to be a random collection of files, but a coherent, recognizable, and useful catalog. Every release is designed to fit within a clear direction, offering products that are different from one another yet tied together by a distinct style.

Compendiums DM Tools

Expansions and support material

Content created to expand the game with concrete tools, additional rules, useful structures, and material that helps the DM better manage campaign flow, table play, and narrative rhythm.

Best for: DMs, long campaigns, and groups looking for more depth and more genuinely useful tools.
Adventures Atmosphere

Mini-campaigns and narrative modules

Short adventures, self-contained arcs, and high-atmosphere modules built to deliver strong, themed experiences that are easy to bring to the table without heavy preparation.

Best for: groups looking for ready-to-run content, special sessions, side quests, and adventures with a strong identity.
Future growth Dedicated lines

Projects that can expand over time

Some publications will begin as individual releases, but may later grow into broader collections, connected materials, or fully dedicated editorial lines as the catalog continues to grow.

Direction: a richer catalog over time, while remaining organized, readable, and coherent as a true editorial brand.
